INV-SQUEUE-06C: Under per-thread durable action lock, preserve selected snapshot, immutable target ID/clientID/action UUID/context/root and payload digest BEFORE any delete. INV-SQUEUE-06D: After confirmed delete=true, durably reserve ONE steer attempt before wire, then turn/steer(threadId,input snapshot,expectedTurnId,clientUserMessageId=action_id (unique transfer action UUID)). Any timeout/crash after reserve is delivery_unknown without automatic retry/requeue/start. A second fresh active-target proof is mandatory after confirmed deletion and before steer attempt reservation/wire; it cannot replace native expectedTurnId. A proven never-wired refusal leaves held; a JSONRPC rejection alone is not generic non-delivery proof. Successful steer ACK sets accepted, does not promise consumed/completed. Exact history proof may promote unknown to accepted; absence never authorizes retry. Late callbacks cannot downgrade state. If pre-delete intent is found after restart, deletion may have occurred: delivery_unknown, no speculative delete/steer.

INV-SQUEUE-06E: Original native row plus Control action receipt are not two pending queues: action payload is recovery data and never automatically dispatched. held/unknown text remains visible via authenticated read for manual recovery; «В черновик» copies the snapshot only, no network action/new UUID until explicit user send. accepted/changed terminal action removes recovery text atomically, retaining digest/idempotency tombstone. Where original Control enqueue UUID is known, status reconciliation accounts for transfer held/unknown/accepted rather than reporting a phantom queued row forever. ## Final integration clarification
A proven related held transfer may derive held status for the original enqueue UUID, without changing its immutable digest or replaying it. Known original qid must match; a missing qid requires unique native clientID and immutable payload proof before binding. Accepted transfer association follows the same rule. Recovery-to-draft may perform bounded read-only reconciliation of at most8 current unknown UUIDs; only matching qid/held proof removes that original blocker. Unrelated unknown remains. Legacy history recent_sends DTO is not broadened; late stale unknown cannot regress a positively held/cancelled shadow. Fresh enqueue never returns held, exact replay after proven transfer can. Confirmed cancellation retires only its associated original pending UI status; copy itself makes no POST or UUID.

## SOURCE safety amendment: action correlation and target proof
The original native queued clientID identifies the removed source row and associated Control enqueue only; it MUST NOT identify the new steer attempt. The steer wire uses clientUserMessageId=action_id. History may promote an ambiguous action only after a durably reserved steer attempt, for UserMessage.clientId=action_id, exact snapshot, and Turn.id=expected_turn_id. Original queued history, other-turn history, or an intent/held action with no reserved steer never proves this transfer accepted. Multiple/conflicting matches remain unknown/conflict with recovery text retained and zero repeated mutation.

When the original enqueue qid is missing after lost ACK, association requires complete unique native clientID and the CURRENT native row's text digest matching the immutable original enqueue. A client-provided snapshot alone is not native binding evidence. A known matching qid still permits the expressly accepted Mac edit/snapshot semantics. Accepted transfer retains a temporary outgoing bubble correlated by action UUID until exact native history merges it once; removal of the queued source must not make the accepted shown text disappear during history lag.
